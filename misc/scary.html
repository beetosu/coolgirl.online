Phantom of the Paradise (1974)

Week of 10/12 | Haunted Level: 10%

A great innovation in camp! One of the defining rock operas. Packed to the brim with jams, inexplicably bird themed, and takes itself just seriously enough to deliver on the emotional beats.

The Exorcist (1973)

Week of 10/19 | Haunted Level: 80%

A horror classic set in Washington D.C.! The spiritual divide between the writer (a devout Catholic), and the director (an Athiest) manifests in the central conflict surrounding a mother who is left feeling helpless as her daughter falls deeper into demonic possession.
